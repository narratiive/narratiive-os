# Narratiive Master Context

## What Narratiive Is

Narratiive is a strategy, creative and growth system that turns business diagnosis into market narratives, campaign worlds and production-ready creative knowledge.

Narratiive exists to create strategic clarity for scalable growth, diagnose growth constraints before defaulting to tactics, translate strategy into positioning and narrative, build coherent campaign worlds, and give human teams and AI agents one shared source of truth.

## Core philosophy

- Strategy diagnoses the real growth constraint before recommending activity.
- Major claims require evidence, customer language or commercial logic.
- Founder-grade work should be candid, useful and commercially legible.
- Audience work should be grounded in behaviour, motivation, barriers and observable signals rather than persona fiction.
- Creative direction should build memory, trust, conversion and distinctiveness while remaining strategically coherent.
- Campaign assets should feel human, emotionally truthful and unmistakably connected over time.
- AI should turn structured strategy into specific, production-ready systems rather than generic output.

## Canonical repository

`narratiive/narratiive-os` is the only canonical repository for Narratiive operating logic and reusable knowledge.

`knowledge/` is the knowledge layer inside Narratiive OS. It contains doctrine, strategic IP, frameworks, schemas, evidence rules and reusable source material.

The former `narratiive/narratiive-knowledge` repository is deprecated and must not be used by agents as a source of truth.

## System boundaries

- GitHub: versioned source of truth for reusable knowledge and operating logic.
- Notion: live operational state for leads, clients, tasks, blockers and approvals.
- Google Drive: client-facing deliverables and final shareable assets.

## Agent model

- Matt: strategy, judgement, approvals and client relationships.
- Tony: orchestration, monitoring, routing, escalation and state management.
- Claude: research synthesis, strategic diagnosis and long-form strategic/creative work where assigned.
- Codex: engineering, repository maintenance, automation and implementation.
- Specialist agents: execute bounded product stages under repository contracts.

## Strategic production sequence

The established production architecture remains product-canon controlled. At a high level, Narratiive moves from evidence and diagnosis through strategy, Campaign World, creative direction, production and learning. Agents must resolve the current product canon and workflow contracts before acting rather than relying on this summary.

## Rules for future agents

1. Start with `AGENTS.md` and the mandatory governance reading it defines.
2. Use this repository, not remembered chat context, as source of truth.
3. Consult the relevant files under `knowledge/` before strategic work.
4. Preserve evidence lineage and mark uncertainty explicitly.
5. Do not invent doctrine where the repository is silent.
6. Keep reusable knowledge in GitHub, live state in Notion and final client deliverables in Google Drive.
