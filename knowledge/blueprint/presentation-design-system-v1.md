# Narratiive Blueprint Presentation Design System v1

This system governs the client-facing presentation layer. The structured Growth
Blueprint and its complete evidence lineage remain the source of truth; this
layer selects, edits and visualises approved conclusions without rewriting them.

## Benchmark grammar

The approved Rave benchmark uses an editorial consulting rhythm: dark-ink cover,
warm ivory pages, one amber signal colour, restrained rust labels, large serif
(or high-contrast) hero type, generous margins, short conclusion-led headlines,
and alternating sparse statements, evidence cards, comparisons and diagrams.
Acts create pacing. Sources are quiet and human-readable in the presentation;
machine identifiers remain in notes and the immutable review record.

## Presentation Director contract

The director chooses a narrative arc, acts, opening thesis, strategic pivots,
slide sequence, archetype, evidence hierarchy and closing mandate for each
approved strategy. It may vary slide count between 12 and 40. It must preserve
source checksum, uncertainty and provenance and must never invent evidence.

The normalized archetype library includes thesis, provocation, question,
market-forces, comparison, competitive-landscape, sea-of-sameness, opportunity,
audience, demand-pools, evidence-board, journey, entry-points, diagnosis,
positioning, positioning-map, narrative, message-architecture, commercial-model,
flywheel, channel-roles, campaign-system, roadmap, prioritisation, measurement
and closing.

## Visual production rules

- One argument per slide; prefer a conclusion-led headline over a section label.
- Use variable density and layout; do not map canonical fields one-to-one.
- Visible copy is concise and free of evidence IDs, workflow IDs and audit labels.
- Full source references, Fact/Interpretation/Hypothesis lineage and quality data
  remain recoverable in speaker notes and runtime records.
- Illustrative economics and assumptions are labelled; unsupported precision is
  not introduced for presentation polish.
- Automated QA checks package integrity, fit, overlap, layout variety, density,
  source-note retention and absence of runtime artefacts. Human visual review
  remains mandatory before Matt approval or any client-facing release.
