# Blueprint Lite

Status: Canonical inbound pre-sales product contract
Version: 4.0
Decision: Matt, 27 August 2026

## Role

Blueprint Lite is the free, personalised follow-up to a completed Growth Diagnostic. It is not the Growth Diagnostic result itself, not a generic lead magnet, and not a shortened paid Growth Blueprint.

Its commercial job is to turn the prospect's own diagnostic inputs plus selective outside-in research into a concise, credible view of potential that earns a Discovery conversation.

The emotional objective is:

> Make the founder more excited about the future of the business, while making it obvious that the most valuable questions still require a deeper engagement.

## Canonical inbound journey

`Growth Diagnostic → Blueprint Lite → Discovery → Growth Sprint → Growth Blueprint → Campaign World`

Definitions:

- **Growth Diagnostic** — instant self-serve result generated from the user's inputs.
- **Blueprint Lite** — free personalised outside-in follow-up generated from the diagnostic plus research.
- **Discovery** — human conversation to test and deepen the hypotheses in Blueprint Lite.
- **Growth Sprint** — paid strategic engagement commissioned by the client.
- **Growth Blueprint** — premium strategic deliverable produced by the Growth Sprint.
- **Campaign World** — downstream creative expression of the approved Growth Blueprint.

`Narratiive Shift` and `Opportunity Card` are not names used in this inbound journey. `Opportunity Card Pipeline` remains an internal legacy/workflow label only where required by the Narratiive Signal outbound system.

## What Blueprint Lite must do

Blueprint Lite must:

1. reflect the prospect's diagnostic inputs accurately;
2. add useful outside-in observation rather than merely restating their answers;
3. identify one strategically interesting growth tension;
4. form one consequential but clearly provisional growth hypothesis;
5. make potential tangible enough to create anticipation;
6. identify the questions that can only be answered through deeper access and Discovery;
7. invite a conversation without presenting a disguised proposal.

It must not:

- pretend to know private commercial facts;
- over-diagnose the business from public information;
- provide a full implementation plan;
- collapse the value of the paid Growth Blueprint;
- manufacture urgency or claim certainty;
- imply that the client has already commissioned work;
- include pricing unless it is part of an authorised post-Discovery proposal.

## Recipient-facing structure

Default length: 5–7 concise designed pages or equivalent reading depth.

### 1. What we heard

Reflect the most material diagnostic signals back to the prospect in plain English. Use their inputs as evidence, not as a score report repeated verbatim.

Answer: what growth problem are they actually describing?

### 2. What we see

Add selective outside-in research: category, competitors, customers, visibility, positioning, current story, distribution or other relevant context.

Separate fact from interpretation. Keep evidence selective.

### 3. The growth tension

State the most interesting contradiction or constraint emerging from the diagnostic plus research.

This should feel sharper than a generic weakness and should not be framed as founder failure.

### 4. The opportunity

Offer one strong provisional hypothesis about where meaningful growth could come from.

The language should be ambitious but qualified: "we think", "one possibility", "the opportunity worth testing".

### 5. What this could mean

Make the opportunity tangible through an initial positioning, narrative, creative or market possibility. This is a glimpse, not the completed strategic answer.

### 6. The questions we'd answer next

List 3–4 consequential questions that require Discovery and/or the paid Growth Sprint. These create the curiosity gap between Blueprint Lite and the full Growth Blueprint.

### 7. Invitation

Close with the distinction:

> This is the outside-in view. A Growth Blueprint goes inside the business — combining commercial reality, customers, category and ambition to establish where growth can come from and how to pursue it.

Invite Discovery to compare notes and test the hypotheses.

## Inputs

Required:

- diagnostic lead ID;
- name and company;
- company website where supplied;
- all Growth Diagnostic answers;
- overall and category diagnostic scores;
- blockage and recommended actions generated by the diagnostic;
- public research sources authorised by the workflow.

Optional:

- previous approved interactions with the prospect;
- public company/founder interviews;
- reviews, retailer listings, public filings, press and category evidence.

## Evidence discipline

Every material external claim must be traceable. Distinguish:

- **Fact** — directly supported by a source or prospect input;
- **Interpretation** — Narratiive's reasoned reading;
- **Hypothesis** — a possibility to test in Discovery or the Growth Sprint.

Do not invent revenue, budgets, priorities, customer motivations, internal constraints or founder intentions.

## Quality gate

A Blueprint Lite may enter human review only when it passes all mandatory checks:

- diagnostic inputs are represented faithfully;
- at least one outside-in observation adds value beyond the diagnostic;
- the growth tension is specific to this company;
- the opportunity is consequential but provisional;
- the document does not accidentally answer the whole paid brief;
- the questions-to-answer-next are commercially meaningful;
- every material fact has evidence lineage;
- tone is warm, direct, founder-friendly and non-patronising;
- no unsupported claim or invented certainty is present;
- the Discovery invitation is clear.

Tony orchestrates, stores, validates and routes. Claude researches, reasons and drafts where the approved workflow assigns it. Tony does not silently rewrite strategic content. Human approval is required before recipient-facing release.

## Relationship to outbound

Blueprint Lite is an inbound product. It must not replace or rename Narratiive Signal, which remains the canonical unsolicited outbound strategic gift.
