## Costing and recommendation
For each proposed engagement record markets, audience groups, business models, evidence volume/quality, research and analytical complexity, tangible outputs, review process, delivery risks and exclusions. Estimate specialist person-days × loaded direct cost per day; include external direct costs, and make risk provision explicit. Loaded cost is internal delivery cost, not client billing rate. Distinguish confirmed costs from provisional estimates, and avoid double-counting salary overhead or contingency. Exclude recoverable VAT consistently; include irrecoverable tax in direct cost.

Provisional target gross margin = 55%, for internal estimation only. Fee floor at that target = estimated direct delivery cost / (1 - 0.55). Gross margin = (fee - direct delivery cost) / fee. This is margin, not markup; the resulting floor is not a universal minimum or an approved quotation. Record what is included in cost and whether the result excludes company overhead. Matt may approve another target for a justified commercial reason.

Recommend ONE exact fixed fee with costing, scope and risk rationale. Explain any rounding or premium, project profitability and the effect of cost overrun/discount. Where rates or scope are uncertain, label the estimate and obtain Matt's decision; never imply actual staffing costs were verified. Verified revenue, if available, provides scale/context, not an automatic price. Unknown revenue remains disclosed and does not justify inventing a figure.

Matt approves strategy, scope, fee, currency, VAT treatment, payment schedule, quote validity, expenses, revision allowance and exclusions. Only the exact approved fixed fee and relevant terms enter the client PDF. Do not display a price band, unapproved recommendation or internal margin calculation to the client. Approval binds to the final terms/PDF version. Separate sending approval remains required.
