# Commercial proposal content contract
Version 2 · applies to the existing post-discovery proposal step

## Evidence intake
Tony combines the current Groundwork version with the completed discovery record and Matt's commercial review. Record client/workspace ID, source IDs/URLs, version, source date, retrieval date, permissions and author. Discovery evidence needs meeting date, participants, transcript/timecodes or approved notes, expressed priorities, constraints, agreed decision and buying signals. A research inference is not something the client said. Client testimony is evidence of the speaker's view, not automatic verification of a financial fact.

Maintain a claim ledger: claim ID; exact claim; source and pinpoint; source date; fact / client-reported / inference / hypothesis / model; confidence and limitation; proposed page/section; permission; reviewer. Reconcile conflicts rather than silently choosing the most convenient value. Missing discovery means an internal example or blocked real proposal, never invented notes.

## Mandatory strategic synthesis
Before drafting, Tony must identify the non-obvious evidenced finding; strategic tension; measurable commercial mechanism; supporting sources/assumptions/open questions; and the specific Fieldwork decisions/outputs. Include one finding a knowledgeable founder could find surprising or worth investigating. Generic differentiation or audience claims fail. Do not invent novelty: if no such finding is defensible, record the research gap and block client release while permitting a visibly internal test.

Groundwork and completed discovery must both be referenced. Reflect what was heard with exact client statements and pinpoints where available. No discovery record means no “what we heard” assertions. A public-source hypothesis may support an internal test but cannot replace discovery for genuine release.

## Minimum content
| Section | Required content | Evidence rule |
|---|---|---|
| Specific headline | Brand-specific commercial tension and decision | Avoid diagnosing a problem unsupported by discovery |
| Why now | Trigger, priority and consequence of inaction | Source urgency; do not manufacture a deadline |
| Opportunity | Strongest plausible audience/brand opening and its commercial mechanism | Separate observation from interpretation |
| Strategic finding and tension | A specific evidence-led observation linked to a meaningful contradiction or unmet need | At least one defensible non-obvious finding; otherwise research gap |
| Commercial value | ONE central mechanism, conservative/upside sensitivity, assumptions/confidence, or precise missing-input request | See OPPORTUNITY_MODELLING.md |
| Fieldwork scope | Opportunity map; strategic brand/go-to-market direction; activation roadmap | Align depth and outputs to agreed scope and active Fieldwork canon |
| Delivery | Seven working days with start conditions | See below |
| Investment | One Matt-approved fixed fee, currency, VAT, payment terms, validity and costs | Bespoke costing and final Matt approval required |
| Next step | Agree scope/terms and commission Fieldwork | Approval is not automatic activation or dispatch |

## Persuasion and writing
Write for a founder deciding whether this question warrants paid strategic attention. Explain the cost of uncertainty without inventing its monetary value. Show understanding of the brand's actual route to demand. Distinguish intended outcomes from guaranteed results. Promise rigorous choices, not revenue growth. Plain English, short paragraphs, meaningful headlines. No invented quotations, no claim of a successful call without the record, no finished strategy given away as if researched. Fieldwork is the paid marketing strategy; later creative development, production, implementation and media are separate commissions.

## Seven-working-day delivery commitment
Required commitment, with proposed contractual wording for Matt’s approval: “We will deliver Fieldwork within seven working days once the agreed scope and terms are accepted, the agreed initial payment is received, and the required information, permissions and stakeholder access are in place. We will confirm the start and delivery dates in writing.”

Working days are Monday–Friday excluding England and Wales bank holidays, unless agreed otherwise. Day 1 is the next working day after Matt confirms the prerequisites complete; Day 7 is the delivery deadline. Delivery means the agreed Fieldwork presentation and supporting evidence/model handoff, including Narratiive's internal review; it does not require the client to approve the strategy within seven days. Required inputs are a bounded intake list agreed before acceptance, not an open-ended delay mechanism. Any later material client dependency is logged promptly with reason, impact and an agreed revised date; no silent clock reset. Subsequent revisions and other phases use separately agreed timing. Do not inherit older 10–15-day or Phase 2 calendar-day promises.

## Acceptance and rejection
Reject client release if findings are interchangeable between brands; scenarios use arbitrary percentages; the fee is a generic band or lacks exact approval; returns are guaranteed; material facts/assumptions lack provenance; Loom Loft design quality is not met; or legacy product labels/unsubstantiated claims remain. Also block release for missing real discovery, key model inputs where a forecast is shown, or incomplete exact-version approvals. An internal fictional example is allowed with visible omissions; it must not be described as commercially approved or release-ready.

## Release gates
Tony prepares the brief and evidence. Matt owns the final design, edits, scope, fee and commercial terms. After approval Tony stores the approved exact-version PDF, checks its identity, and attaches it to the existing prepared commercial email. Final PDF/design approval does not authorise sending. Separate send authorisation must identify recipient, subject/body and that exact attachment. Any change to the PDF or email invalidates the relevant approval. This kit does not change current runtime behaviour or authorise any Origin contact.

Approval of a reference example never substitutes for the client-specific exact-fee, terms and final PDF approvals.
