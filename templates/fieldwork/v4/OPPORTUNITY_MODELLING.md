# One-opportunity financial modelling standard
Version 2 · implements COMMERCIAL_SPECIFICATION.md sections B and C

Choose ONE primary growth mechanism supported by the Groundwork and discovery evidence. State why it matters and what alternative mechanisms were excluded; do not present or sum a generic collection of uplift levers. The full Fieldwork strategy can explore wider options, but the two-page proposal models one opportunity.

## Evidence hierarchy and inputs
Prefer (1) client-confirmed operating/financial data, (2) verifiable company disclosures, (3) relevant sourced external benchmarks with transfer limitations, (4) explicitly labelled assumptions requiring validation. Keep definition, value/range/unit, baseline period, cohort/market, source/pinpoint/date, confirmation status, method and uncertainty for every material input. Client-confirmed assumptions remain assumptions. Resolve conflicts. Never treat a public offer as evidence of sales effectiveness.

## Formula selection
- E-commerce conversion: eligible sessions × incremental conversion probability × net average order value. Define qualified traffic and session-to-order attribution consistently.
- Retention: eligible customers × incremental retained purchases × net revenue per purchase; reconcile customer/cohort overlap.
- Subscription: subscriber cohort × incremental retained paid periods × net subscription revenue per period; adjust churn, timing and overlapping cohorts.
- Average order value: eligible expected transactions × incremental net order value; account for mix, discounts and demand effects.
- Wholesale: qualified incremental partner prospects × conversion probability × onboarding probability × annual mature net revenue per partner × first-horizon exposure/ramp/survival factors. If “partners” already means won and onboarded accounts, never apply conversion again. Use monthly cohorts where available; distinguish additional prospects from an uplift in wins in the same pipeline.
- Other: derive the actual business-specific formula, definitions and counterfactual.

No defensible denominator, baseline or driver means no financial forecast. Name the opportunity and exact data Fieldwork would quantify; do not fill the space with total-revenue percentages or fictional numbers.

## Scenario display and precision
Use ONE central evidence-based scenario as the dominant visual and a concise conservative/upside sensitivity. Show which causal input changes, its source-supported range, confidence and gaps. Sensitivities are not forecasts with probabilities attached unless justified; no predetermined uplift ladder. Do not reverse-engineer an attractive result. Central assumptions must be independently justified; source-backed ranges may be correlated, so do not blindly combine every optimistic extreme.

## Economics and attribution
Define baseline/counterfactual, horizon, revenue recognition, net-of-VAT/returns/discounts basis, cohort overlap and cannibalisation. Distinguish incremental revenue from gross and contribution profit. Include marginal service/delivery costs, acquisition/implementation costs, sales-cycle delays and capacity where material. Existing pipeline wins under the baseline are not incremental. Do not claim causation from before/after movement alone. Label model precision appropriately and retain unrounded calculations internally. Fieldwork does not guarantee modelled growth; subsequent implementation requires separate investment.

## Origin application

Actual central/conservative/upside results are unavailable because prospects, baseline wins, account economics, onboarding and ramp data are absent.
