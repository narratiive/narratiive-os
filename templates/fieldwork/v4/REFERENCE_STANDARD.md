## What approval covers
The reusable structure, content requirements, modelling discipline and visual language are the approved reference. This does not make fictional figures into genuine Origin data or approve the exact £9,800 fee for a real engagement. Each future proposal requires its own evidence, tailored audience/positioning/activation argument, supported model (or explicit limitations), deliberate imagery and Matt's final design/commercial approval.

## Exact-fee release requirement
A real client proposal cannot be released until Matt has explicitly approved the exact fee, currency, VAT basis, scope and terms for that specific client and PDF version/hash. Approval of this reference structure or the fictional example is insufficient. The current £9,800 excluding VAT remains provisional in commercial-approval-record.json. No “INVESTMENT” heading or successful render implies approval. Tony may subsequently store and attach only the approved exact-version PDF. Sending requires separate approval of the final document, recipient and message.

No Tony operational workflow, active reconciliation state, email infrastructure or live release mechanism was changed. These are authoring/review standards, not a claim that an unimplemented runtime gate is now executable. No broad tests were run; only targeted PDF, image-identity and content-preservation checks.
