# Supervised Growth Blueprint real-client runbook

Status: launch preparation. This procedure does not authorise client delivery, production deployment or external contact.

## 1. Qualify the decision

Owner: Matt.

Accept an engagement only when one accountable founder has a consequential growth decision, the organisation can supply material commercial evidence and the scope can be resolved by a Blueprint rather than implementation work.

Record the decision question, intended users, confidentiality, source permissions, deadline, commercial scope and authorised client approver. Create an isolated workspace and client identity before any source enters the system.

Gate: Matt approves the engagement and commercial terms.

## 2. Collect and reconcile the evidence

Owner: Research Analyst, supervised by the operator.

Minimum commercial pack:

- revenue, orders, VAT, discounts, refunds and returns by agreed period;
- sessions, conversion definition and funnel stages;
- product and channel contribution after variable costs;
- acquisition spend, attributable revenue and creative cost;
- repeat purchase, retention and cohort contribution;
- stock, fulfilment, service and team capacity;
- committed and possible implementation investment.

Minimum strategic pack:

- founder interviews and current strategy;
- product, proposition, brand and campaign materials;
- permitted customer research, reviews and interview access;
- competitor set and market sources;
- relevant legal, operational and reputational constraints.

Assign one of the five evidence classes to every material input. VERIFIED CLIENT requires an authorised document identity. VERIFIED EXTERNAL requires publisher, date and URL. Record conflicts and gaps; do not average them away.

Gate: client finance owner or founder confirms definitions and material reconciliations.

## 3. Build the commercial decision case

Owner: Strategy Director.

Separate the verified baseline from scenario assumptions. Show the mechanism for each lever, the ramp, incremental contribution, implementation investment, capacity and cannibalisation. Use downside, base and upside cases. Reconcile bridges. State where payback or return cannot be calculated.

Gate: Matt reviews the commercial logic before it enters the executive opening.

## 4. Diagnose the constraint and advantage

Owner: Strategy Director.

Name one binding constraint, its causal mechanism and the evidence that supports it. Write the counter-test that would make the team reject it. Define a proposed advantage only as strongly as customer, competitor and capability evidence allows.

Gate: constraint and advantage survive a red-team review and have named validation plans.

## 5. Develop strategy and creative proof

Owners: Campaign World Generator and Creative Director.

Create three prioritised moves linked to a commercial lever, owner, trade-off and test. Develop at least one tangible creative territory that shows how the strategic advantage could appear. Label untested work CREATIVE ILLUSTRATION. Complete rights review before any external use.

Gate: Matt selects the strategic and creative route for production; this is not client release approval.

## 6. Produce the Blueprint

Owner: Creative Director, coordinated by Tony.

Produce the opening five and seven chapters through the existing presentation pipeline. Keep the locked 30-slide canon unchanged until its separate governance decision. Ensure the founder can state the opportunity, constraint, moves and immediate decision after the first five slides.

Gate: package validation and visual review pass with no clipped copy, unsupported claim or unapproved asset.

## 7. Independent QA

Owner: Quality Reviewer who did not author the relevant specialist output.

Challenge source permissions, evidence classification, model arithmetic, causal logic, scenario language, creative relevance, implementation feasibility and presentation clarity. Return specific revisions to the owning specialist. QA never silently rewrites specialist work.

Gate: QA returns PASS_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW or blocks the package.

## 8. Matt approval and client handoff

Bind the Blueprint, deck, PDF, ledger and QA checksums to the approval request. Matt records approve, request revision or block. Any revision creates a new version and a new approval decision.

After approval, present the Blueprint live. Distinguish verified findings, scenarios, hypotheses and illustrations. Record the founder's decisions, rejected assumptions and requested corrections. Do not imply implementation approval.

## Expected timetable

| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Qualification, contracting and intake | 1-3 working days | 2-3 hours |
| Evidence verification and research | 3-5 working days | 3-5 hours |
| Commercial model and strategic diagnosis | 2-3 working days | 3-4 hours |
| Strategy and creative future | 2-3 working days | 3-4 hours |
| Production, QA, approval and handoff | 2-4 working days | 3-5 hours |
| Total after complete intake | 10-15 working days | 14-21 hours |

These are planning assumptions to validate in a paid pilot, not a delivery promise.

## Stop conditions

Stop and escalate when source permission is unclear, client identity conflicts, finance definitions do not reconcile, commercial downside is unacceptable, contribution or cost evidence is missing, creative rights are uncertain, specialist and reviewer independence is unavailable or Matt has not approved the exact version.
