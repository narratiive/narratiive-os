# Tony COO attention correction — 5 November 2026

Owning stream: Worker Infrastructure (runtime, scheduled services, managed Tony
workspace instructions and tests). No change to specialist ownership or approval
authority.

## Incident and correction

The scheduled material watcher bypassed the agency attention projection and
unioned all Mission Control blocker/approval strings. Its renderer printed ten
internal identifiers and an omitted-item count. The conversational prompt could
not change this deterministic output.

Both escalation services now select through the agency projection and briefing
attention policy. Scheduler composition supplies the same suppression and prior
briefing decision pipeline as the executive command service. Routine GitHub
checks/conflicts stay in Mission Control. Workflow approvals require a matching
business summary; old workflow versions and explicitly labelled internal test
records remain directly queryable but do not become founder decisions. A real
business delivery block remains eligible, including non-engineering failures.

Messages explain the work, its consequence and a recommended next step. The
message envelope retains whole items and provides an explicit route to the
remaining queue rather than cutting off text. Human-readable company names are
preserved even when also used as entity IDs. The managed Tony operating
principles now state the COO judgement standard explicitly.

Promised-work failures name the deliverable, distinguish quality correction from
execution recovery, recommend the responsible next step, and explicitly avoid
claiming a retry or correction has started. Existing idempotent delivery markers,
quality checks, exact-version human gates and append-only records are retained.

## Verification

Regression cases reproduce raw GitHub alert noise, historical approvals,
current-version selection, genuine business interruption, opaque identity
fallbacks, bounded complete messages and truthful quality-failure explanations.
The live preview uses the actual scheduled composition with delivery disabled;
it is not a Telegram send or proof that paused specialist work has recovered.

This change improves operational judgement and delivery. It does not establish
that every existing historical workflow is repaired or that all agency sources
are connected. Tony must continue to distinguish missing evidence from a healthy
business state.
