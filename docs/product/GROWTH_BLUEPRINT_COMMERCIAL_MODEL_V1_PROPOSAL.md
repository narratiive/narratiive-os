# Commercial opportunity model — worked fictional fixture
Status: demonstration only, not client findings or promised performance. 2026-10-13.

## Fictional FIELDWORK assumptions
Business annual turnover £3,000,000, of which online direct-to-consumer sales £1,200,000 and other channels £1,800,000 (assumed, not independently verified).
Online baseline: 600,000 annual sessions × 2.0% order conversion × £100 average order value = £1,200,000.
Illustrative base scenario: 750,000 sessions × 2.4% conversion × £105 AOV = £1,890,000.
Incremental annual online revenue £690,000, or +57.5%. This is not incremental company turnover unless the other £1.8m remains constant and channel displacement is absent.

## Additive bridge (sequential, non-double-counting)
Traffic effect: (750,000-600,000) × 0.020 × £100 = £300,000.
Conversion effect: 750,000 × (0.024-0.020) × £100 = £300,000.
AOV effect: 750,000 × 0.024 × (£105-£100) = £90,000.
Sum = £690,000. Order of attribution is a modelling convention; sensitivity analysis must test interactions and dependencies.

## Scenarios
Downside: 630,000 sessions × 2.1% × £102 = £1,349,460 (+£149,460).
Base: 750,000 × 2.4% × £105 = £1,890,000 (+£690,000).
Upside: 810,000 × 2.6% × £108 = £2,274,480 (+£1,074,480).
All values hypothetical. They are neither sector benchmarks nor forecasts. Changes are simultaneous annualised run-rate inputs, not automatically realised revenue during the 12-month ramp.

## Critical missing inputs
- Verified revenue by channel, returns, discounts, VAT, gross margin, shipping and inventory capacity.
- GA4 sessions, conversion definition, channel cohort and paid/organic split; ecommerce platform order exports.
- Repeat purchase, new customer acquisition, customer cohorts and incremental contribution margin.
- Marketing spend by channel, CAC/MER/ROAS definitions, brand/search demand and creative performance.
- Seasonality, growth ramp, stock-outs, cannibalisation and scenario costs.

## Investment decision
Gross incremental revenue alone cannot justify spend. For each scenario calculate contribution = incremental net sales × incremental contribution margin - incremental marketing and fulfilment costs - fixed implementation costs. Calculate payback, downside cash exposure and capacity constraints. No ROAS or profitability claim without channel costs and attribution methodology.

## Validation gates
Reconcile baseline with transaction exports; validate traffic and conversion denominators; run sensitivity tornado; add cost/benefit; validate founder feasibility and capacity; disclose evidence gaps before presenting any 12-month claim.
