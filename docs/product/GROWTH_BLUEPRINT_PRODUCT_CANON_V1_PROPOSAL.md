# Narratiive Growth Blueprint — Product Canon v1.0 (proposed)

Status: PROPOSED CANON — requires Matt's approval and merge before it supersedes existing locked Blueprint specifications.
Date: 2026-10-13
Owner: Narratiive / Matt
Audience: founder-led growth businesses, approximately £0.5m–£50m annual revenue.

## Product promise
Narratiive finds the next meaningful growth opportunity for a business, quantifies its plausible commercial value, explains what prevents the business from capturing it, and demonstrates the strategic, creative and activation choices required to pursue it.

The Growth Blueprint is a persuasive, evidence-led commercial argument, not a chronological research report. Lead with the destination and opportunity, then earn belief through evidence, choices, creativity and a practical plan.

## Fixed narrative spine — seven chapters
1. **The Opportunity** — Where could this business be in 12 months? Growth scenario, financial bridge, founder-facing ambition.
2. **The Constraint** — What is the principal bottleneck, and why? Evidence-backed diagnosis, alternatives considered, confidence.
3. **The Advantage** — What differentiated customer/category/brand advantage can be exploited? Research, competitors, audiences, distinctive assets.
4. **The Strategy** — What must change? A single thesis, clear strategic choices, priorities, trade-offs and audience proposition.
5. **The Creative Future** — What could this look and feel like? Creative platform, distinct territories, selected direction, visual and verbal expressions.
6. **The Growth Engine** — How does it acquire, convert and retain customers? Channel roles, media, activation, funnel, investment and measurement.
7. **The Next 12 Months** — What happens next? Phased roadmap, owners, costs, tests, milestones, leading indicators, decision gates.

Chapter order is the default narrative contract. Content, page count, models, research methods and creative expression are adaptive to the business. A chapter can be compact but may not be omitted without documented justification.

## Mandatory opening five slides (story function, not universal literal headlines)
1. **The destination:** Bold, specific, founder-relevant 12-month ambition. Hook immediately. Scenario, not promise.
2. **The numbers:** Transparent current-to-potential commercial bridge, with verified vs assumed inputs and downside/base/upside sensitivity.
3. **The constraint:** One sharply expressed diagnosis with evidence and what would falsify it.
4. **The moves:** Three prioritised changes linked to the constraint, commercial levers and trade-offs.
5. **The proof / reveal:** A compelling preview of the research, strategy and creative future that earns the remainder of the story.

Do not force a numeric claim into slide 1 where evidence is insufficient. State a conditional opportunity instead; never fabricate baseline revenue, conversion, ROAS, leads or market size.

## Commercial model rules
Choose the economics appropriate to the business: ecommerce (traffic × conversion × AOV; repeat), lead-gen (qualified leads × close rate × value), subscription (acquisition × retention × ARPU), or other defensible unit economics. Reconcile modelled channel revenue to company revenue where applicable. Show time horizon, assumptions, sensitivity, feasibility, investments, constraints and measurement plan. Separate incremental revenue, profit and return; avoid double-counting correlated effects. No guaranteed 12-month outcomes.

## Evidence integrity
Label every material datum: VERIFIED CLIENT, VERIFIED EXTERNAL (source/date), MODELLED, ASSUMPTION or CREATIVE ILLUSTRATION. Charts must trace to a reproducible table and formula. Record uncertainty, evidence gaps, contradictions, and validation steps. No invented customer quotes, case studies, market data or proof. Illustrative fictional examples must be unmistakably marked.

## Visual standard
Premium investment-case clarity meets high-end creative agency craft. Use strong hierarchy, concise headlines, charts that prove an argument, relevant diagrams, distinctive iconography and coherent visual worlds. No generic stock visuals or decorative charts. Creative territories must follow the strategic thesis. Provide editable source and rendered review deck where feasible. Accessibility and legibility matter.

## Specialist ownership
Research Analyst: source-backed evidence pack and confidence.
Strategy Director: growth thesis, diagnosis, scenarios and choices.
Creative Strategist: creative platform and territories.
Creative Production: visual world and executions.
Media & Activation: channel/investment/test plan.
Measurement & Effectiveness: KPIs, assumptions, sensitivity, evaluation.
Independent Quality Reviewer: adversarial review of evidence, maths, strategic distinctiveness, creative coherence and actionability.
Tony: orchestration, continuity, progress and exception handling; not substitute author or final approver.
Matt: final commercial and client-facing approval.

## Quality gates
Pass only when: (1) the opening proposition is commercially meaningful; (2) all material claims are sourced or labelled; (3) financial arithmetic reconciles; (4) the constraint is diagnostic, not generic; (5) recommendations connect to measurable levers; (6) creative derives from strategy; (7) activation and 12-month plan are feasible; (8) output is visually coherent and client-ready. Fail to the responsible specialist with precise deficiencies; preserve human approval for external use.

## Reference demonstration
Create a clearly fictional founder-led business with plausible economics. First produce five high-fidelity opening slides and the underlying assumption/evidence ledger. Then expand to the seven chapters and one coherent creative territory. Gold-standard output is the acceptance fixture for specialist-agent production; it is not evidence about real clients.
