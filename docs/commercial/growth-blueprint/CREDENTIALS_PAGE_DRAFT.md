# Why Narratiive for a Growth Blueprint

Status: internal draft. No client result or case-study claim may be added without verified permission and evidence.

## One argument across commercial and creative decisions

Narratiive is a strategic growth studio for founder-led businesses. We bring commercial modelling, strategy and creative development into one decision system so the recommendation does not stop at analysis and the creative work does not float free of economics.

## What the process protects

- **Evidence integrity:** every material claim carries one of five explicit evidence classes.
- **Commercial honesty:** scenarios remain separate from forecasts; costs and margins determine whether revenue has value.
- **Strategic focus:** the Blueprint names the binding constraint and forces prioritised trade-offs.
- **Creative ambition:** a strategy must produce a distinctive, tangible future rather than a mood-board adjective list.
- **Human judgement:** independent QA can recommend; Matt approves the exact client-facing work.

## What exists today

Narratiive has implemented a complete fictional vertical slice covering structured intake, evidence lineage, commercial scenarios, seven chapters, a five-slide executive opening, creative proof, editable presentation production, rendered QA and checksum-bound approval.

The FIELDWORK sample is synthetic demonstration evidence. It is not a client result, endorsement or published case study.

## What a client contributes

The strongest Blueprint depends on founder access, permitted customer and competitor evidence, reliable commercial data and a willingness to decide. Narratiive will identify uncertainty rather than decorate it.
