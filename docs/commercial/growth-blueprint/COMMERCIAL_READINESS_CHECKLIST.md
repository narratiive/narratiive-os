# Growth Blueprint commercial readiness checklist

The offer is not commercially releasable until every required item has an owner, evidence and approval.

## Offer and pricing

- [ ] Matt approves the target client and disqualifying criteria.
- [ ] Matt approves the fixed fee or bounded pricing test.
- [ ] Payment timing, VAT treatment, expenses and cancellation terms are approved.
- [ ] Included revisions and scope-change triggers are approved.
- [ ] Delivery promise has been tested against at least one supervised pilot.

## Legal and rights

- [ ] Proposal terms receive legal review.
- [ ] Confidentiality and data-processing responsibilities are clear.
- [ ] Client-supplied data and asset permissions are recorded.
- [ ] Generated and third-party asset licences support the intended use.
- [ ] IP ownership and usage rights are approved.

## Evidence and delivery

- [ ] Mandatory data request and evidence permission form are ready.
- [ ] Commercial definitions reconcile with the client's finance owner.
- [ ] Research sources, dates, URLs and limitations are captured.
- [ ] Independent QA is assigned before production starts.
- [ ] Matt approval is bound to the exact final checksums.
- [ ] Final handoff clearly separates recommendations, scenarios and implementation decisions.

## Sales materials

- [ ] Offer one-pager approved.
- [ ] Opportunity Card template approved.
- [ ] Proposal template approved.
- [ ] Website copy approved and evidence-checked.
- [ ] Credentials claims and case-study permissions verified.
- [ ] Fictional FIELDWORK sample approved for the intended audience.

## Operations

- [ ] Named operator can reproduce the runbook.
- [ ] Secure client workspace and access rules are tested.
- [ ] Backup, versioning and immutable lineage are verified.
- [ ] Delivery calendar includes client delays and revision capacity.
- [ ] A no-go process exists for missing evidence, unsafe economics or unresolved legal risk.
