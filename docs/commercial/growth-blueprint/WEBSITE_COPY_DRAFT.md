## The problem

Most founder-led businesses have more possible growth moves than they can fund. New products, new audiences, a sharper proposition, more media or a brand change can all sound plausible. The difficult work is deciding which move has evidence behind it, what must be true commercially and what to stop doing.

## The product

The Growth Blueprint is a persuasive investment case, not a generic research report. It connects five things founders usually receive separately:

- the opportunity and its plausible commercial value;
- the constraint that blocks it;
- the advantage the business could own;
- the strategic and creative choices required;
- the evidence-gated plan for the next 12 months.

## What you will see first

The first five slides answer the questions that matter in the room:

1. Where could the business go?
2. What could the opportunity be worth?
3. What currently prevents growth?
4. Which strategic moves are required?
5. What could the future look like?

## How Narratiive works

Specialist AI workflows support research, commercial modelling, strategy, creative development, production and QA. Tony orchestrates the work. Humans supervise the evidence, judgement and creative choices. Matt approves every commercial recommendation and client-facing Blueprint before delivery.

## Commercial discipline

Scenarios are not forecasts. Revenue is not profit. Every material claim is labelled as verified client evidence, verified external evidence, modelled, assumption or creative illustration. When evidence is missing, the Blueprint says so and turns the gap into a decision or test.

## Who it is for

The Growth Blueprint is for a founder-led business facing a consequential growth choice and willing to share the evidence needed to resolve it. It is especially useful when the team has momentum but lacks one commercially rigorous argument that aligns product, brand, marketing and investment.

## FAQ

### Is this a brand strategy?

Brand may be part of the answer, but the Blueprint starts with a growth decision and commercial evidence.

### Is the commercial model a forecast?

No. It presents scenarios, assumptions, sensitivities and missing inputs so the founder can judge what deserves verification.

### Do you implement the plan?

Implementation is optional and separately scoped after the Blueprint. The Blueprint must stand on its own.

### Does AI make the decision?

No. Specialist AI workflows increase production depth and speed. Humans retain commercial judgement, quality control and final approval.
