## The engagement

Narratiive will build a supervised Growth Blueprint that identifies the next meaningful growth opportunity, quantifies plausible value under explicit scenarios, diagnoses the binding constraint, recommends strategic moves and makes the creative future tangible.

The engagement answers one agreed commercial question. It does not promise growth or replace legal, tax, accounting, investment or regulated financial advice.

## Scope

1. Founder discovery and decision framing.
2. Evidence intake and permission review.
3. Customer, market and competitor research within the agreed sources.
4. Commercial scenario modelling with visible assumptions and limitations.
5. Constraint, advantage and strategic recommendation development.
6. One developed creative territory.
7. Growth engine and 12-month decision roadmap.
8. Independent QA, Matt approval and final presentation handoff.

## Client inputs required

- revenue, orders, returns, discounts and VAT treatment by agreed period;
- channel spend and attributable revenue;
- product-level gross margin, fulfilment, payment and variable service costs;
- web sessions, conversion definitions and funnel exports;
- retention, repeat purchase and cohort data where relevant;
- customer research, reviews and permitted interview access;
- competitor set, existing strategy, brand and creative assets;
- operational capacity, stock, delivery constraints and planned investment;
- one authorised decision-maker and permission to use each supplied source.

Missing inputs remain explicit assumptions or blockers. Narratiive will not invent them.
