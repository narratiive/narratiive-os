# Narratiive Growth Blueprint

## Find the growth move worth making next

Founder-led businesses rarely lack ideas. They lack a clear view of which opportunity matters, what blocks it and what deserves investment now.

The Growth Blueprint is a supervised strategic engagement that turns company evidence, market context and commercial assumptions into one persuasive investment case. It gives the founder a point of view they can challenge, a future they can see and a practical sequence for proving it.

## The decision it helps you make

- Where could the business go next?
- What could that opportunity be worth under explicit scenarios?
- What constraint prevents the business from capturing it?
- Which strategic moves deserve attention, and which should wait?
- What could the business look and feel like if the strategy works?
- What evidence must change before you invest further?

## What you receive

- a five-slide executive opening;
- a seven-chapter Growth Blueprint;
- a transparent commercial scenario model;
- an evidence and assumptions ledger;
- one developed creative territory;
- a growth engine and 12-month decision roadmap;
- an editable presentation and review PDF;
- an independent QA report and final human approval gate.

## How it works

Narratiive collects the evidence, interviews the founder, builds and challenges the commercial case, develops the strategy and creative future, then subjects the output to independent QA. Specialist AI workflows increase depth and speed. Humans retain judgement. Matt approves every commercial recommendation and client-facing version before delivery.

## Timing and price test

Indicative delivery is two to three weeks, subject to complete data access and interview availability. The current **£3,000-£6,000 range is a pricing hypothesis**, not an approved public offer. Final scope, fee and payment terms require Matt's approval before this page is used externally.

## Good fit

The Growth Blueprint is designed for a founder-led business with a real growth decision, access to commercial evidence and willingness to make trade-offs. It is not a generic research report, finished campaign production, financial advice or a promise of growth.
