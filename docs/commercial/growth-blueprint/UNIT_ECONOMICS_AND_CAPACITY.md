The model excludes company overhead, sales time, failed proposals, tax, travel, legal review and optional implementation.

## Contribution sensitivity before overhead

| Price | Contribution at £1,930 cost | Margin | Contribution at £1,110 cost | Margin |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| £3,000 | £1,070 | 35.7% | £1,890 | 63.0% |
| £5,000 | £3,070 | 61.4% | £3,890 | 77.8% |
| £6,000 | £4,070 | 67.8% | £4,890 | 81.5% |

These figures are not profit. They indicate whether the proposed fee can plausibly fund supervised judgement and production before overhead.

## Capacity implication

At 14-21 human hours per Blueprint, one accountable lead with 60 protected delivery hours per month could supervise approximately two to four concurrent engagements, depending on client delays and revision load. Launch planning should assume **two paid pilots per month** until measured delivery evidence supports more.
