## Central creative proposition

**Cats don't perform affection. Evidence is the only language they speak — and feeding is the first place an owner ever gets fluent in it.**

Internal line: *"The proof is in paying attention."*

## Human insight (claims-qualified)

Cat owners get almost none of the constant, legible feedback dog owners take for granted — no tail, no whole-body enthusiasm on cue. What they get instead is rare: a slow blink, a chosen lap, a cat settling somewhere specifically near them. Feeding is one of the few daily moments where an owner actually watches their cat closely rather than past it.

This is a claim about attention, not about health. The treatment does not assert, depict, or imply that switching food changes a cat's coat, energy, weight, or behaviour — that is a nutritional/behavioural claim requiring its own evidence and legal sign-off, and none has been supplied. The imagery shows an ordinary domestic moment of an owner noticing their cat, independent of any product-efficacy claim.

## The two concepts

- **Concept A — "The Evidence":** an intimate, observational moment of domestic stillness between owner and cat.
- **Concept B — "Caught Looking":** the cat breaking the fourth wall, mid ordinary moment, as if it's just noticed it's being watched.

One idea at two speeds — DTC narrative depth (A) and three-second shelf legibility (B) — tied together by the shared visual system below, not two different photographic treatments.

## Shared visual system

| Element | Rule (applies to both concepts) |
|---|---|
| **Light** | Single-source natural window light only, 4500–5200K, soft-to-hard directional. No studio seamless, no coloured rim light, in either concept. |
| **Palette** | Warm, desaturated domestic naturalism — oatmeal, butter, raw linen, graphite. One accent colour reserved for brand red, **provisional pending real KatKin brand guidelines**. |
| **Lens/grain** | Consistent 85mm-equivalent compression and true photographic grain in both — no CG smoothing, no stock-photo clarity. |
| **Signature device — "The Look"** | Both concepts resolve on the cat making direct eye contact with camera. In A, it's the closing beat after a quiet moment; in B, the entire image is built around it. The one recurring, ownable visual cue meant to recur across every future execution. |
| **Composition rule** | Subject held off-centre in the lower two-thirds; generous negative space in the upper frame reserved for lockup/copy, in both. |
| **Product presence rule** | Product, where present, stays marginal and out of hero focus — never styled as a product shot, never implying a before/after or causal outcome. |
| **Environment rule** | Real, lived-in home textures only — no styled sets, no seamless backdrops. |

## Final Higgsfield prompts (two, production-ready for internal rehearsal only)

**Prompt 1 — Concept A, "The Evidence"**

- **Subject and scene:** A short-haired domestic cat resting on a sunlit kitchen floor beside a plain ceramic bowl (contents and brand unspecified), in a quiet, unstaged moment. On the final beat, the cat turns its head and makes direct eye contact with camera.
- **Composition:** Mid-close, 85mm-equivalent compression, shallow depth of field. Cat held in the lower-right third of frame; generous negative space upper-left reserved for a future lockup (none generated). Low camera angle, near floor level.
- **Lighting/photographic treatment:** Single hard window-light source, 4500–5000K, visible dust motes in the light shaft, true photographic grain, no CG smoothing.
- **Brand constraints:** No logo, wordmark, or packaging design generated. Bowl is plain/unbranded placeholder. Colour palette: warm desaturated domestic tones; no red accent in this frame (reserved for Prompt 2) — provisional, pending real brand kit.
- **Aspect ratio / intended use:** 4:5 — primary for DTC social feed and email hero; internal rehearsal asset only, not cleared for external use.
- **Negative constraints:** no studio backdrop, no coloured rim/edge light, no HUD/sci-fi overlays, no anthropomorphising (costumes, human expressions), no on-image text or claims copy, no glossy 3D-render look, no visible branded packaging, no second animal or human in frame.

**Prompt 2 — Concept B, "Caught Looking"**

- **Subject and scene:** The same cat, seated on a laundry pile on a bedroom floor, caught mid-turn as it looks directly into camera — the entire image built around that single moment of eye contact.
- **Composition:** Mid-distance, eye-level, deliberately slightly off-balance framing (one ear/shoulder near frame edge) to read as candid rather than posed. Generous negative space above, same lockup-reservation rule as Prompt 1.
- **Lighting/photographic treatment:** Soft overcast window daylight, low contrast, true-to-life colour, faint motion blur in the fabric to reinforce an unposed moment. Same lens/grain family as Prompt 1.
- **Brand constraints:** No product, logo, or packaging in frame — this is the emotional payoff image, intended to sit beside packaging, not contain it. No colour accent beyond the shared desaturated palette — provisional, pending real brand kit.
- **Aspect ratio / intended use:** 1:1 — primary for retail shelf-talker/packaging-adjacent photography and paid social; crops to 9:16 for Reels/TikTok and vertical OOH. Internal rehearsal asset only.
- **Negative constraints:** no flash or studio lighting, no seamless/coloured backdrop, no staged "pet photography" posing, no text/claims overlay, no competitor or branded packaging visible, no human in frame, no exaggerated/cartoonish expression.
