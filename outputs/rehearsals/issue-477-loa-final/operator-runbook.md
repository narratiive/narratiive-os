# Supervised Growth Blueprint operator runbook

## Before intake

Confirm the client identity, owner, permissions, evidence sources and exact commercial policy. Keep the opportunity at Needs Review until the inbound record and sender are verified. Never convert a rehearsal, self-message or synthetic record into a real opportunity.

## Discovery and follow-up

Use the Fireflies meeting ID as the immutable source reference. Tony may prepare a summary and follow-up, but the operator must compare the draft with the transcript, confirm recipients and keep it in Draft until approval. If Gmail draft API scope is unavailable, use the supervised UI step and verify DRAFT is present and SENT is absent.

## Commercial close

Use £5,000, 50/50, one consolidated revision, VAT conditional, with production and activation separate only after Matt confirms the policy. Do not mark Won until scope, commercial terms, contract and payment/PO evidence all satisfy the authoritative close gate.

## FIELDWORK production

Replace assumptions with authorised client evidence. Classify every material claim as VERIFIED CLIENT, VERIFIED EXTERNAL, MODELLED, ASSUMPTION or CREATIVE ILLUSTRATION. External category facts never verify client performance. Run the existing supervised Blueprint pipeline, inspect the five-slide opening and all seven chapters, render the exact PDF/PPTX and retain hashes.

## Creative production

Start only from the approved strategy and Campaign World. Record asset provenance and distinguish generated illustration from real product/customer evidence. Matt confirms provenance, product accuracy, the exact brief, budget and final assets.

## Release and activation

Independent QA must pass, then Matt approves the exact checksum. Client delivery, production booking, campaign creation, publication and spend are separate actions with separate approval evidence. A readable advertising account is not authority to build or publish.

## Recovery rules

- On duplicate inbound, reuse the existing Notion record and suppress a second alert.
- On provider timeout, record failure honestly and retry only an idempotent or read-only step.
- On delayed specialist work, wait for verified completion; never infer a result.
- On missing credentials or authority, mark the stage BLOCKED and continue independent stages.
- On any uncertain external write, verify provider state before retrying.
