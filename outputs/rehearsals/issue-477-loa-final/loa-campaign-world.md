# LOA Campaign World — A Brighter Pour

Status: **internal creative illustration; not approved for production or publication**

## Strategic handoff

LOA may have a noticeable bottle but has not proved an ownable occasion or trial-to-repeat system. The world should make one product ritual unmistakable and measurable; it must not imply that the commercial hypothesis is already true.

## Creative north star

**Make the pour the moment the scene wakes up.**

## Visual world

- Begin in familiar, slightly flat real-life light.
- Introduce coral through the bottle and liquid, not decorative clutter.
- Keep the gold cap, cream lettering, condensation and bubbles tactile.
- Pair with food and hands only where rights and product accuracy are cleared.
- Let sound carry recognition: cooler hum, cap release, pour, glass and conversation.

## Campaign territories

### 1. The brighter lunch

A late desk lunch, corner-shop sandwich or shared plate becomes deliberate through the LOA pour. Test occasion comprehension and sample-to-site capture.

### 2. Bring the colour

The bottle is the only vivid object in an ordinary social setting. Test brand attribution and asset recognition without lifestyle overclaim.

### 3. One more round, differently

A second social round is expressed through the serve, without claiming equivalence to alcohol or a health benefit. Test credibility and legal acceptability before development.

## Repeatable system

Every execution must include: a recognisable ordinary pause, the LOA bottle, an audible opening, the pour, one appetite cue, and the line **POUR BRIGHTER**. Optional end line: **MAKE THE PAUSE MATTER**.

## Channel translations

- Selective wholesale: cooler, menu and table prompts tied to the occasion.
- Sampling: one-city food/design/culture partners with permission and capture.
- DTC: mixed-case trial route and replenishment prompt.
- CRM: occasion reminder, feedback, second-purchase and referral tests.
- Paid social: only scaled after source occasion and repeat economics are evidenced.

## Human gates

Strategy approves evidence lineage. Legal approves claims and rights. Matt confirms asset provenance/product accuracy and approves the selected world, exact production brief, budget, final assets and any publication or media commitment.
