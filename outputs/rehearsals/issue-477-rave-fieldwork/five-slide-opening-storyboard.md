# FIELDWORK: Rave Coffee five-slide opening

Status: internal review draft. Unsolicited exercise; not approved for Rave Coffee. Public facts require revalidation before use.

## 1. The current position

**The pieces of a growth system already exist. The system itself is still hidden.**

Rave already combines approachable speciality coffee, a broad range, recurring purchase routes, education and wholesale service. The opportunity is not to invent another offer. It is to make those pieces behave like one clear customer journey.

Open inputs: channel economics, cohort behaviour and operational capacity.

## 2. The credible 12-month opportunity

**Turn more first orders into more valuable relationships.**

Qualified reach × first-order conversion × 90-day repeat × value progression = more contribution.

This is a lever model, not a forecast. Rave's internal baselines must replace every variable before a value or target is stated.

## 3. The growth levers

**Make the journey easier to enter, choose, stay in and progress through.**

- Enter: recruit at the upgrade moment.
- Choose: reduce uncertainty at the first decision.
- Stay: make replenishment and subscription feel useful, not binding.
- Progress: give customers a visible route from dependable favourites to discovery.

Proof and operational reliability gate all four levers.

## 4. The audience opportunity

**The Upgrader is the priority hypothesis — not a finished persona.**

The most useful audience is defined by a commercial moment: someone ready to leave supermarket coffee behind, often because their interest, routine or equipment has changed. This audience hypothesis must be validated with CRM, conversion and primary-research evidence before scale.

## 5. The strategic direction

**OWN THE UPGRADE.**

Make serious coffee easy to enter, easy to choose and worth staying for. Organise acquisition, choice architecture, replenishment and discovery around one progression. Do not scale an arbitrary media budget; prove each lever, then decide where investment earns the right to grow.

The editable `.pptx` and review `.pdf` contain the designed opening.
