## Rubric

Scores assess whether the work: builds one cumulative argument; tells a founder something consequential; distinguishes verified evidence, interpretation and hypothesis; makes the commercial mechanism legible without invented forecasts; could only belong to Rave; creates a memorable future; uses direct English; and ends in choices with trade-offs.

## What the historic Claude work does better

- It commits to an ownable idea and gives creative development a strong centre of gravity.
- It behaves like a senior editorial commission, selecting and sequencing rather than displaying every analytical field.
- It makes the founder feel the distance between today's business and a more distinctive future.

Its score is capped by evidence weaknesses. Several market, audience and performance statements are not safely traceable, and the Campaign World turns illustrative media allocations and targets into apparently precise recommendations without an internal commercial baseline.

## What the controlled direct commission proves

- With the same frozen evidence and same model, a founder-oriented commission produces a clearer sequence and stronger decisions.
- It refuses to invent a 12-month revenue forecast and instead exposes the variables Rave would need to supply.
- It labels the Upgrader audience as a hypothesis and makes validation part of the strategy.

It is still too risk-led and operational in places. Its raw output overweights a pod-claim inconsistency and historic accounts; the curated review deck therefore uses current public facts only, removes superseded financial figures and turns the opportunity into a simpler commercial lever model.

## What Tony currently does better

- More complete source lineage.
- More explicit uncertainty and trade-off recording.
- Better protection against fabricated commercial claims.

Those strengths belong backstage. In the founder-facing argument they produce repetition, equivocation and too many co-equal choices. The result is diligent but less compelling.

## Conclusion

The model is not the quality bottleneck. The commission and acceptance contract are. Preserve Tony's evidence controls, but require the system to pass a short founder argument before it is allowed to expand or render.
