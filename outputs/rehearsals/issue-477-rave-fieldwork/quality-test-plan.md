# Proposed FIELDWORK argument-gate tests

These tests are proposed for the smallest repair. They are not yet production gates.

1. Exactly five opening moves: current position, 12-month opportunity, growth levers, audience opportunity and strategic direction.
2. Every move includes evidence, insight, commercial implication and decision.
3. No material claim lacks one of: VERIFIED CLIENT, VERIFIED EXTERNAL, MODELLED, ASSUMPTION or CREATIVE ILLUSTRATION.
4. A MODELLED opportunity exposes variables and source requirements; it is never called a forecast without approved client data and assumptions.
5. Each move adds a new fact, implication or choice; repeated claims fail.
6. The strategic direction is traceable to the first four moves.
7. Substituting a competitor's name without changing the argument fails the Rave-specificity check.
8. Arbitrary budgets, targets and unsupported audience attitudes fail.
9. Current public claims must carry a retrieval date; archived inputs must be labelled for revalidation.
10. Human commercial approval remains required before full presentation generation or client-facing release.
