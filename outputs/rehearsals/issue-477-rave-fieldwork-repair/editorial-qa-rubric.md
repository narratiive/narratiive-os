# FIELDWORK founder-argument editorial QA

Status: experimental gate, not the production default.

## Passing standard

The gate reviews the argument before any page or presentation schema exists. Every axis must pass; `recommendation: advance` cannot override a failed axis or missing contract field.

| Axis | Pass condition | Typical rejection |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | Direct language; every sentence carries an identifiable meaning. | Jargon, inflated phrases, unexplained abstractions or advice that sounds polished but says little. |
| Specificity | The recommendation depends on the client's actual assets, constraint and commercial moment. | A competitor could replace the client's name without changing the advice. |
| Evidence | Every material move points to classified source references. | Unsupported customer attitudes, silent assumptions or evidence references outside the supplied pack. |
| Commercial relevance | A named mechanism connects behaviour to commercial value without inventing a forecast. | Activity lists, channel recommendations without economics, arbitrary budgets or precise outcomes without baselines. |
| Founder usefulness | Each move ends in a consequential decision or validation requirement. | Interesting analysis that leaves the founder with no choice to make. |
| Repetition | The five moves accumulate; each adds a new fact, implication or decision. | The central thesis repeated under different headings. |
| Quantitative discipline | Currency and percentage claims appear only when verified or explicitly modelled. | Unsupported financial precision presented as a target or forecast. |

## Five required moves

1. Current position
2. Commercial opportunity
3. Growth levers
4. Audience opportunity
5. Strategic direction

Each move requires a headline, argument, commercial consequence, founder decision and evidence references. The five moves are reasoning objects, not presentation layouts.

## Evidence classifications

- VERIFIED CLIENT
- VERIFIED EXTERNAL
- MODELLED
- ASSUMPTION
- CREATIVE ILLUSTRATION

Scenarios must remain labelled as scenarios. A model with missing client inputs is not a forecast.

## Failure behaviour

The quality result records every failed check and detailed editorial findings. Failed model output is retained as an immutable attempt artefact, the workflow blocks, and presentation production remains unavailable. Revision creates a new version; it never overwrites the failed attempt.
