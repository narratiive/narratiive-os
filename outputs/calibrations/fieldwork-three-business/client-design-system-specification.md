# FIELDWORK client-specific design specification

Status: candidate design specification. It does not replace any client's own brand standards and does not create a full SKETCHBOOK production system.

## Shared FIELDWORK grammar

- Format: editable 16:9 presentation, one argument per slide.
- Grid: 64 px outer margin, strong left alignment and generous negative space.
- Hierarchy: 42–64 pt decision headline; 18–30 pt argument; 9–12 pt evidence and status.
- Evidence: evidence status and open inputs remain visible; detailed citations sit in editable speaker notes.
- Colour roles: paper/neutral carries reasoning, dark ground carries the growth mechanism, client signal colour marks direction, client evidence colour marks proof and uncertainty.
- Sequence: current position, commercial opportunity, growth levers, audience opportunity, strategic direction.
- Asset rule: one useful hero image at most; no decorative dashboards, invented charts or unlabelled AI imagery.
- Status rule: every candidate remains visibly internal and unapproved until the human gate is complete.

## Rave Coffee — preserve the original example language

Design idea: **a clear path through serious coffee**.

- Ink `#17130F`; Paper `#F5EFE3`; Signal Yellow `#FFCA05`; Evidence Cobalt `#1738A3`; Roast `#6B351F`.
- The yellow path remains the memory device for customer progression and founder choice.
- Editorial coffee imagery is tactile, naturally lit and leaves useful negative space.
- FIELDWORK uses paper, ink and cobalt evidence cues. SKETCHBOOK may make the path and photography more expressive, but must retain the same type hierarchy, crop behaviour, footer and status language.
- The existing internal coffee still life is a creative illustration, not a Rave-owned asset.

## KatKin — same product grammar, different client world

- Ink `#181619`; Warm Paper `#FFF4EA`; Signal Coral `#FF5B4D`; Evidence Violet `#6546D7`.
- Cat portraiture provides emotional immediacy; the dark scientific ground is reserved for mechanisms and proof.
- Coral carries action and transition. Violet carries evidence, uncertainty and founder decisions.
- Avoid generic wellness gradients, veterinary theatre or unsupported health imagery.

## The Yardstick Agency — service-led proof

- Ink Navy `#13263A`; Paper `#F3F0E8`; Signal Gold `#F2B138`; Evidence Teal `#28736B`.
- The primary visual metaphor is a decisive route through a change point, expressed through edge bands, lines and ordered typography.
- The design must work without stock boardrooms, adviser handshakes or client logos.
- Gold carries the buying moment. Teal carries diagnosis and evidence.

## FIELDWORK ↔ SKETCHBOOK continuity

FIELDWORK explains the commercial choice. SKETCHBOOK makes the approved choice tangible. A client pair should remain recognisably related when the product labels are hidden: palette, type hierarchy, motif, image treatment, evidence status and footer remain shared. SKETCHBOOK can increase image scale and expressive copy, but it cannot convert an assumption into a fact or imply approval.

## Editable and review outputs

Each calibration opening is retained as an editable PowerPoint file and a rendered PDF review copy. The editable file is the primary artefact. The PDF proves the intended review rendering, not cross-application editability.
