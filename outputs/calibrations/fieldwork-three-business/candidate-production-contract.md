# FIELDWORK founder-argument gate — candidate production contract

Status: opt-in candidate only. The existing production workflow remains the default. Promotion requires Matt's explicit approval.

## Purpose

Establish one commercially useful founder argument before a presentation schema can shape the reasoning. The gate is a thinking contract, not a deck template.

## Required inputs

1. A bounded strategic question and agreed scope.
2. A research ledger whose records use an allowed evidence classification and retain source references.
3. A strategic synthesis stating the commercial problem, opportunity and implications.
4. A commercial growth relationship with known inputs, missing inputs and explicit assumptions.
5. Client context stating whether first-party data is available.

Public evidence must never be presented as client evidence. Assumptions must remain assumptions. Missing first-party data must remain visible.

## Required reasoning output

The specialist must return one coherent founder argument containing:

- a clear business opportunity;
- a priority audience opportunity;
- one strategic recommendation, its trade-offs and the next founder decision;
- a commercial growth mechanism with two to four levers;
- known and missing inputs;
- an evidence basis linked to the ledger;
- exactly five ordered argument moves: current position, commercial opportunity, growth levers, audience opportunity and strategic direction.

The mechanism must state `scenario_not_forecast=true`. The reasoning stage must not return layouts, page furniture or presentation metadata.

## Automated gates

The candidate fails closed if a mandatory field is absent, the five moves are missing or out of order, an evidence reference cannot be resolved, a recommendation is generic, a founder decision is absent, unsupported financial precision appears, or the language fails the editorial checks for clarity, specificity, evidence, commercial relevance and usefulness.

Passing automation means the response is complete enough for human judgement. It does not mean the strategy is correct.

## Human gate

Every accepted argument stops at `awaiting_approval`. Matt must approve, revise or reject the argument before presentation production. Approval must consider:

- whether the insight is commercially material;
- whether the recommendation is sufficiently distinctive;
- whether the evidence supports the confidence of the language;
- whether the trade-off is real and acceptable;
- which missing first-party inputs must be obtained before external use.

## Presentation handoff

Only an approved argument may be translated into the five-slide opening. The five moves stay in order. Slide production may improve expression but must not introduce a new recommendation, a forecast, an investment budget or unreferenced evidence.

## Promotion criteria

Before this contract becomes the production default:

1. Matt reviews the three-business calibration and the preserved original Rave benchmark.
2. At least one supervised paying-client rehearsal demonstrates that first-party evidence can be incorporated without weakening the argument.
3. The independent quality reviewer confirms that a passing response is not merely schema-complete.
4. Full regression, rollback and deployment review pass on the exact candidate commit.

## Rollback

The workflow remains opt-in. Rollback is to stop commissioning `fieldwork_founder_argument_experiment`; no production workflow, manifest or locked specification needs to be changed.
