# Rave Coffee evidence revalidation

Captured: 18 October 2026  
Status: public evidence only; internal unsolicited exercise.

## Revalidated current evidence

- Companies House lists Rave Coffee Limited as active. Accounts to 31 December 2025 were filed on 8 September 2026. Revenue, profit, channel mix and unit economics were not extracted or verified.
- Rave's current Coffee Club publicly offers Traditional, Discovery and mixed subscription routes from £9.95, with tracked delivery and educational framing.
- Subscribe & Save publicly offers a 10% recurring discount. The wider public range includes blends, single origins, rare releases, pods, gifts, equipment, wholesale and cold brew.
- Rave Wholesale publicly combines coffee with machines, installation, training, servicing and ongoing support.
- The CBI's August 2026 UK coffee study describes premiumisation and speciality-market growth. The British Coffee Association reports high category consumption and faster growth among 18–34 year olds. These are market indicators, not proof of Rave demand.
- Pact currently presents a visible three-tier subscription ladder. Grind combines coffee formats with lifestyle-led subscription cues. Origin and Union foreground provenance, quality or discovery. Prices and pack sizes are not treated as a like-for-like index.
- Rave publicly states that it gives 1% of sales to environmental causes. The cumulative figures on the page are not dated or methodologically reconciled, so they are not used as impact proof.

## Current strategic interpretation

Rave's public offer supports a reasonable hypothesis that accessible breadth could become a guided customer progression. It does not establish which entry route produces conversion, 90-day repeat, contribution or movement into a higher-value relationship.

## First-party evidence unavailable

- qualified traffic and acquisition source;
- first-order conversion by entry route;
- 90-day repeat and subscription retention;
- contribution margin by product and channel;
- product progression and cohort value;
- wholesale retention, service capacity and economics;
- validated customer segments and decision moments.

No external use should present the upgrader audience or guided progression as validated until those inputs are supplied or researched directly.
